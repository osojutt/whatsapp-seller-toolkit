# WhatsApp Seller Toolkit

Free, open-source tools for Facebook → WhatsApp sellers (Cash on Delivery e-commerce).

If you run Facebook ads and take orders on WhatsApp, you answer the same questions
hundreds of times a day: *What is the price? Is delivery free? Is Cash on Delivery
available? Can I open the parcel before paying?* This toolkit turns those answers
into one tap.

## What's inside

Open `index.html` in any browser — no installation, no sign-up, no server needed.

1. **WhatsApp Order Link Generator** — enter your number, product and price, and get
   a `wa.me` link with the order message pre-filled. Put it in your Facebook ad or
   page and customers land in your WhatsApp with the order already typed.
2. **Quick Replies** — ready-made answers (English + Arabic) for the questions
   customers ask every day: price, free delivery, Cash on Delivery, open-parcel
   before payment, delivery time, asking for order details, and order confirmation.
   Product name and price are filled in automatically.
3. **Order Note** — one clean format to save every order the same way.

## Why I built this

I sell through Facebook ads and WhatsApp myself, and I personally reply to
400–1,000 customers a day to take their orders. I built the tool I needed:
the repeated answers, ready in one click, in the two languages my customers use.

## Use it / contribute

- Use it free for your own shop — that's what it's for.
- Found a reply you answer every day that is missing here? Open an issue or a
  pull request with the question and your answer.
